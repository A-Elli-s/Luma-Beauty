BEGINNER WIG GUIDE

Glueless wigs for beginners: what to know before you buy.

Glueless wigs are designed to be worn without relying on adhesive for everyday hold. For a first wig, focus on fit, texture, lace area, density and how much styling you actually want to do.

Start with the look you will actually wear

A short curly bob can be easier to work into an everyday routine, while longer loose curls create more styling options. Consider your normal schedule and maintenance preferences before choosing based on length alone.

Pay attention to lace and density

Lace construction affects the parting and styling area. Density affects how full the hair appears. More is not automatically better; choose the combination that matches the finish you want.
