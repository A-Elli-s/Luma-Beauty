PRIVACY

Simple, transparent site practices.

This page explains how Luma Beauty's current affiliate links, creator application, referral attribution, and analytics may involve visitor information.

Information collected

If you apply to the creator program, the application requests your name, email, social profile, platform, approximate audience size, country, and information about your content. Applications are submitted to Luma Beauty's application service for review.

Affiliate links

Product links redirect through Awin to participating retailers. Awin and retailers may process referral information and use cookies or similar technologies under their own privacy policies. Luma Beauty may receive commission reporting associated with eligible purchases.

Analytics and cookies

The site may use website analytics to understand visits and interactions. If a visitor arrives using a creator referral code, Luma Beauty stores that code and a timestamp in browser local storage for up to 30 days; browser storage can be cleared through browser settings. The creator application service may also record referral visits. Browser settings and applicable third-party privacy controls may offer additional choices.

Updates

This privacy information may change as the site adds new features or services.
