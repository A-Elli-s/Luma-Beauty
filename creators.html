PROGRAM BASICS

Before creators start promoting

How would a sale be credited to me?

Approved creators receive unique tracking links such as ?ref=creatorcode. Luma stores the creator attribution for a limited attribution window, while final sale confirmation comes from the participating affiliate network or retailer.

How much would I earn?

The creator's percentage will be specified in the creator agreement. It is calculated from eligible commission actually received by Luma Beauty, after returns, cancellations, network adjustments, taxes, or other excluded amounts described in the agreement.

Can I promote any product?

Creators should only promote products and links that are approved for the program and must follow the advertising, endorsement, affiliate-network, retailer, and platform rules that apply.

Do I need to disclose that I may earn money?

Yes. Creator content must use clear disclosures appropriate for the platform and applicable advertising rules.
