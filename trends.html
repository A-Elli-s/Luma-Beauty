LIPS

How to Get the Blurred Lip Look

Start with a stain or soft lipstick concentrated near the center of the lips, then diffuse the edge instead of drawing a hard outline. A liner close to your natural lip tone can add structure underneath, while a small amount of gloss or oil can shift the look from soft matte to dimensional shine.

EYES

Brown Mascara vs. Black Mascara

Brown mascara creates softer contrast and works especially well for understated daytime makeup. Black mascara remains the stronger choice when you want maximum definition. Carrying both gives shoppers a simple way to change the intensity of a look without changing the rest of the routine.

FACE

Why Berry Blush Is Everywhere

Berry tones can create anything from a subtle flushed effect to a deeper editorial cheek depending on formula and application. Cream and liquid textures make the color easy to sheer out, which helps the trend translate into everyday makeup.

COMPLEXION

Skin Tint vs. Foundation

Skin tints generally emphasize lighter coverage and a more natural finish, while foundations typically offer more coverage options and finish control. Skin tint is useful for shoppers who want an easy base, while foundation remains better suited to people who want more correction or customization.
